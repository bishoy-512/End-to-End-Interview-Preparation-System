# End-to-End AI Interview Preparation System

An AI-powered interview preparation system designed to help job seekers prepare for job interviews through personalized questions, interactive mock interviews, answer evaluation, and actionable feedback.

## 💡 Project Idea

The system analyzes a job description or job posting URL to understand the role requirements and generate interview questions tailored to the position and the candidate's skill level.

Using **Agentic AI**, the system guides users through a complete interview preparation journey, from understanding job requirements to evaluating their answers and recommending learning resources.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Job Analysis:** Extracts job requirements, responsibilities, and required skills from a job description.
- **Personalized Interview Questions:** Generates interview questions based on the job role and its requirements.
- **Interactive Mock Interviews:** Simulates interview sessions with questions ranging from easy to hard.
- **Answer Evaluation:** Evaluates user answers and provides detailed feedback.
- **Performance Report:** Identifies strengths, weaknesses, and areas that need improvement.
- **Learning Recommendations:** Suggests relevant learning resources to help users improve their skills.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. The user provides a job posting URL or job description.
2. AI agents analyze the job requirements and identify the key skills needed.
3. The system generates personalized interview questions.
4. The user answers the questions during a mock interview.
5. AI agents evaluate the answers and provide feedback.
6. The system generates a final report with improvement recommendations and learning resources.

## 🧠 Agentic AI Approach

The project uses a multi-agent approach in which specialized AI agents collaborate to perform different tasks, such as job analysis, interview planning, answer evaluation, and learning recommendations.

## 🎯 Project Goal

To make interview preparation more personalized, practical, and accessible by helping candidates understand job requirements, practice effectively, and improve their chances of performing well in real interviews.
